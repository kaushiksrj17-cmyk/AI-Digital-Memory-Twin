# Research Objectives

## Evidence-Grounded, Privacy-Preserving Multimodal AI for Interactive Digital Memory Preservation

### Primary Research Objective
To develop, validate, and evaluate a computational multimodal architecture capable of organizing, grounding, and presenting heterogeneous biographical memory artifacts (photographs, voice recordings, personal writings, official documents) in an interactive digital twin format while strictly preventing fabricated conversational assertions, preserving user privacy, and enforcing non-deceptive ethical boundaries.

### Ten Core Research Pillars
1. **Preserving Heterogeneous Personal Memories**: Ingesting unstructured text, scanned handwritten letters, vintage audio tapes, family photo albums, and official vital records into a unified semantic schema.
2. **Processing Different Media Types**: Cross-modal alignment connecting voice transcripts, visual scenes, and OCR extracts to shared historical events.
3. **Connecting Memories with People, Events, and Places**: Establishing a spatio-temporal knowledge graph that links individuals, kinship networks, and milestone dates.
4. **Retrieving Relevant Evidence**: Sub-second semantic search and hybrid keyword-vector retrieval grounded in exact document coordinates.
5. **Generating Evidence-Grounded Responses**: Synthesis pipelines that formulate answers strictly from preserved records rather than model parametric memories.
6. **Expressing Confidence**: Categorizing every assertion into an explicit three-tier confidence scale:
   - `GREEN`: Verified Evidence
   - `YELLOW`: Supported Inference
   - `RED`: Unknown / Unsupported
7. **Showing Source Provenance**: Exposing explicit citations, document folio numbers, and recorded timecodes for every factual statement.
8. **Detecting Unsupported Claims**: Implementing guardian verification mechanisms to detect and suppress hallucinations or confabulations.
9. **Protecting Private Personal Data**: Complete data isolation on private local storage with zero-knowledge encryption architecture and granular consent scoping.
10. **Ethical Interactive Memorial Experience**: Fostering healthy, respectful memorialization that explicitly rejects deception, refuses consciousness simulation, and transparently discloses AI authorship.
