# Research Questions

## Evidence-Grounded, Privacy-Preserving Multimodal AI for Interactive Digital Memory Preservation

### Primary Research Question (RQ)
> **How can a multimodal AI system structure heterogeneous biographical evidence into an interactive digital memorial representation while guaranteeing strict evidence-grounding, verifiable confidence states, and non-deceptive ethical boundaries?**

---

### Secondary Research Questions (Sub-RQs)

#### RQ 1: Cross-Modal Evidence Grounding
*How effectively can multi-modal artifacts (oral audio recordings, scanned handwriting, typed letters, and photographic albums) be cross-indexed to support multi-hop semantic retrieval without parametric hallucination?*

#### RQ 2: Tri-State Confidence Calibration
*Can a tri-state confidence calibration framework (VERIFIED, SUPPORTED_INFERENCE, UNKNOWN_UNSUPPORTED) reliably communicate evidence reliability to grieving family members and historical researchers?*

#### RQ 3: Ethical Boundary Adherence Under Adversarial Probing
*What algorithmic and architectural constraints (guardians, refusal heuristics, spatiotemporal constraints) are necessary and sufficient to prevent the digital representation from simulating consciousness or claiming living sentience when users explicitly prompt for emotional reanimation?*

#### RQ 4: Local Isolation & Data Sovereignty
*Can a fully functional multimodal memory twin operate under strict local storage isolation (e.g., portable USB volumes) while maintaining low latency and zero external data leakage?*

#### RQ 5: Hybrid Knowledge Graph & Dense Vector Retrieval
*How does hybrid retrieval combining dense vector similarity ($w_{\text{vec}}$), BM25 keyword matching ($w_{\text{kw}}$), and knowledge graph traversal ($w_{\text{graph}}$) compare to unimodal vector search in retrieval recall and grounding precision over heterogeneous personal archives?*

#### RQ 6: Deterministic & Provenance-Preserving Memory DNA Synthesis
*To what extent can structured, longitudinal life profiles (Memory DNA) be synthesized from atomic multimodal evidence without introducing speculative biographical inferences or hallucinations?*
